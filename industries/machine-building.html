Industry

Machine Building

Engineering capacity for equipment that must build, assemble, and ship cleanly.

Dutam supports OEMs and machine builders with accurate 3D models, production drawings, and CAD workflows that keep design intent intact from concept through fabrication.

The challenge

Machine packages stall when models, BOMs, and shop drawings drift apart, or when every revision becomes a manual redraw. Clients need partners who understand both the product and the CAD process behind it.

What we deliver

  • Equipment and machinery 3D models ready for design review
  • Manufacturing and assembly drawing sets
  • CAD customization for repeat components and routines
  • Documentation standards that survive the next revision cycle

Outcomes

  • Fewer shop queries on fits, clearances, and assembly sequence
  • Faster turnaround when design capacity is constrained
  • Cleaner handoff between design, detailing, and production
