Industry

Construction

Structural clarity where steel, RCC, and building systems must stay aligned.

Dutam helps construction and structural teams coordinate models, steel packages, and BIM interfaces so site and fabrication teams work from one coherent story.

The challenge

Construction programs lose time when structure, MEP, and architectural intent are not coordinated early. Clash noise and incomplete detailing show up as RFIs and rework on site.

What we deliver

  • Structural steel detailing packages to project standards
  • BIM coordination support across RCC, HVAC, MEP, and structure
  • Connection-aware drawing sets for fabrication
  • Model and document control that tracks revision history

Outcomes

  • Earlier clash visibility before site commitment
  • Fabrication packages shops can follow without guesswork
  • Clearer multidisciplinary interface ownership
