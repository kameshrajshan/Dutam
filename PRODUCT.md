# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary visitors are engineering and project buyers evaluating Dutam as an outsourced CAD partner. They arrive while scoping machine building, construction, oil and energy, or plant work, and need to judge whether Dutam can deliver to their standard, software, and schedule. Job candidates and existing DPMS clients are not the primary audience.

## Product Purpose

This site exists so a buyer can judge fit, then send a project enquiry. Success is a qualified discovery request (24-hour reply, NDA available). Careers and DPMS login are present but are not the main job of the site.

## Positioning

Dutam sells CAD engineering that fabrication and plant teams can issue without guesswork: 3D modelling, steel detailing, BIM, CAD automation, documentation, and plant design, delivered on time, on standard, and on spec. Senior engineers who have seen field failure work with younger engineers trained on current tools (AutoCAD, HiCAD, Revit). Neighboring shops cannot truthfully claim that pairing plus the published standards set (AISC, EN, DIN, IS) and the named European client record.

## Operating Context

Buyers compare drawing rules, interface discipline, and software fit before they talk. Existing clients open DPMS at `https://dpms.svtawny.com:85/home`. Work is judged against member/connection packages, coordinated models, and documentation that survives handover. The company is registered in Chennai with a Salem branch and serves European and other international projects in English plus Dutch, German, and French.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static multi-page site: home, about, services hub, four industry pages, six capability pages, contact, careers, privacy.
- Languages: English, Dutch, German, French (`?lang=`, cookie, localStorage, `navigator.languages`).
- Project, contact, and career forms are client-side only until a backend exists. They must not imply a network submit.
- Login is an external DPMS link, not an in-site account system.
- Open: no listed job openings; careers is a general application.

## Product Principles

1. A buyer should be able to judge software, standard, and sector fit before they speak to anyone.
2. Published facts stay published: identity, services, locales, stats, named quotes, and the DPMS login.
3. The enquiry path is the conversion; other doors (careers, login) stay secondary and honest.
4. Forms must not pretend a server is there until one is.
5. Future copy stays specific to CAD delivery; it does not invent a broader product.
