Capability

3D Modelling & Drafting

Models and drawing sets fabrication and assembly can follow without guesswork.

Dutam builds accurate 3D models and production drawings for equipment, structures, and plant components. Visualization, detailing, and issue then happen from one controlled source.

The challenge

When models are incomplete or drawings lag the model, shops invent missing information. That inventiveness becomes rework, scrap, and schedule slip.

What we deliver

  • 3D models for equipment, structures, and plant components
  • Production and assembly drawing sets
  • Revision-controlled issue packages
  • Alignment to client CAD standards and naming conventions

Outcomes

  • Earlier design visualization with fewer ambiguities
  • Drawing sets that match the model at issue
  • Less back-and-forth between design and fabrication
