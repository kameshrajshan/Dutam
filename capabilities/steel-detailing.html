Capability

Steel Detailing

Connection-aware structural packages to the standards your project requires.

Dutam details structural steel from design intent through a fabrication package. Member and connection drawings follow AISC, Eurocodes, DIN, IS, and project-specific rules.

The challenge

Incomplete connections and unclear bolt/weld intent force fabricators to interpret. Interpretation is where cost and schedule risk hide.

What we deliver

  • Member and connection detailing packages
  • Shop-oriented drawing sets and material lists
  • Standards-aligned detailing (AISC, Eurocodes, DIN, IS, and others as specified)
  • Coordination with architectural/structural design intent

Outcomes

  • Packages fabricators can price and build with fewer RFIs
  • Clear connection logic before steel is cut
  • Standards compliance documented in the deliverable set
