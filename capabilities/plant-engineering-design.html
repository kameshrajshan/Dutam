Plant Engineering Design

Multidisciplinary plant design with careful interface discipline.

Dutam supports plant layout, modelling, and coordinated documentation. Plant work succeeds when interfaces between disciplines stay clear through every change.

The challenge

Plant design is an interface problem as much as a modelling problem. When piping, structure, and equipment change independently, the GA becomes fiction.

What we deliver

  • Plant layout and GA development support
  • Equipment and piping arrangement modelling
  • Cross-discipline interface coordination
  • Issue-ready documentation packages

Outcomes

  • Layouts that remain coherent after change orders
  • Better constructability and access planning
  • Documentation ready for client review and handover
