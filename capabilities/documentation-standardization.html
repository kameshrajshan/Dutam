Capability

Documentation & Standardization

Templates and workflows built so the next revision does not start from scratch.

Dutam sets up templates, drawing standards, and PDM/PLM-minded workflows so files stay findable, controlled, and reusable across projects and teams.

The challenge

Without standards, every project reinvents naming, title blocks, and revision habits. Knowledge leaves with people; quality becomes personal, not systemic.

What we deliver

  • Drawing and model templates aligned to client rules
  • Naming, revision, and issue workflows
  • PDM/PLM-friendly folder and metadata practices
  • Handover packs that remain usable after project close

Outcomes

  • Faster onboarding of new engineers onto a project
  • Fewer lost files and orphaned revisions
  • Consistent client-facing document quality
