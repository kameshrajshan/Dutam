Who We Are

Our team brings together engineers with over a decade of industry experience and younger engineers trained on current tools like AutoCAD, HiCAD, and Revit. The senior engineers understand how projects fail in the field. The junior engineers know the fastest way to build and deliver in the software. Working together, they cover both sides of what a good engineering project needs: sound judgment and current method. You get that combination on every project we take on.

Services

Dutam Engineering offers six core services that cover the full range of CAD and engineering design work.

Dutam builds accurate 3D models and production drawings for equipment, structures, and plant components. Visualize early, detail precisely, and issue drawing sets that fabrication and assembly can follow without guesswork.

Dutam builds custom tools, scripts, and interfaces so engineers spend time on decisions, not repeated clicks. Automation covers common CAD platforms and connects design work to the systems your team already uses.

Dutam details structural steel from design intent through a fabrication package. Member and connection drawings follow AISC, Eurocodes, DIN, IS, and the other standards your project requires.

Dutam keeps architectural and construction disciplines in one model story. Coordination across structure, HVAC, and MEP reduces clash noise before it becomes a site delay.

Dutam sets up templates, workflows, and drawing standards so files stay findable, controlled, and reusable. The system is built so the next revision does not start from scratch.

Dutam supports multidisciplinary plant layout, modelling, and coordinated documentation. Plant work succeeds when interfaces between disciplines stay clear through every change.
